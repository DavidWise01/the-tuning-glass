What is real here. LIT The two estimators are the actual ones. The logit lens (nostalgebraist, 2020) is softmax( LayerNorm(hℓ) · WU ) — unembed a mid-stack residual with the model’s own output head. The tuned lens (Belrose et al., 2023) inserts a learned per-layer affine first: softmax( LayerNorm(Aℓhℓ+bℓ) · WU ). Every rank, probability, and KL on this page is computed from the vectors, live, each time you move the slider — nothing is baked.
What is a figure. FIG The residual trajectory hℓ, the unembedding WU (V=24), the final-LN parameters, and the tuned affines Aℓ,bℓ are a seeded AMBER toy field, built so the naive read carries a known basis-drift the tuned read removes. That is a demonstration of the method, not a measured result — the toy’s bias is one I put there so the correction has something to correct.
Where it stops — the wall. WALL A real logit-lens / tuned-lens finding needs a real transformer’s residuals and a tuned lens actually trained on that model; magnitudes and even the sign of the gap vary by model, layer, and prompt. Crucially: a lens read is a projection, not the model’s thought. The raw logit lens is provably biased (that is the whole point), and the tuned lens is a trained probe, not the model’s belief. Decodability is not the causal computation, and none of “the answer climbing” is a moment of deciding or a token watching itself think — it is a statistical relation between a vector and an output head. There is no one inside the glass.
